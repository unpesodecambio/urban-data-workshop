# Module 0 - Previous exploration

---

## Objective

 - Set up tools and procedures exploring how the bike lines habe been implemented in Cali, since they where drafted in 2015's POT, to today's bike lanes.

---

## Question

 - How have the bike lines have grown in relation of what was first drafted on 2015's POT?

---

## Data

 - POT 2015
 - Cali Bike Lanes, 2025

---

## Results

 - There's only a few missing Bike Lines that have still to be implemented; on the other side, many new Bike lines have been constructed that are not on the original 2015 plan.
